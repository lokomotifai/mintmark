## Name status

Mintmark is a provisional working name. Trademark screening at TURKPATENT,
EUIPO, USPTO, and WIPO is not complete, and namespace ownership on PyPI is not
confirmed. Until that screening completes and the name is frozen, treat every
use of the name in this repository as provisional.

## Fair community use

You may use the name to refer to this project truthfully. For example, to say
that your tool reads Mintmark manifests, that you publish a dataset minted with
Mintmark, or that you wrote a tutorial about Mintmark.

You may not use the name or a confusingly similar name as the name of your own
product or service, in a way that suggests the project endorses you, or in a
modified distribution presented as the original.

## Attribution inside minted data

Every minted dataset carries its provenance in `MINTMARK.json`. That manifest is
the attribution mechanism, and it stays intact regardless of the dataset
license. Removing or forging it misrepresents where the data was struck.
