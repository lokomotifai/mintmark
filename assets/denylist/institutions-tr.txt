# Matching is folded, contiguous-phrase, and word-boundary aware.
#
# Two rules shape these entries, and both were learned from a miss:
#   Legal-form and category words are stripped, because every fictional bank
#   this project generates contains the word 'bank'. But a connector inside a
#   name is kept, because matching is contiguous and 'yapi kredi bankasi' never
#   appears inside 'yapi ve kredi bankasi'.
#   An entry whose distinctive part is short or is an ordinary Turkish word keeps
#   the whole name. 'is' is the brand of Turkiye Is Bankasi and also the ordinary
#   word for work; 'hayat' means life. Alone, either fires on ordinary prose.
